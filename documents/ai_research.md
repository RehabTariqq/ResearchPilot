# AI Research

Artificial intelligence research includes areas such as machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, and intelligent agents.

Modern AI applications often combine language models with external tools or information sources. Retrieval systems are one example of this approach.

Research assistants can use retrieval to locate relevant information before generating a response. This can make the process more transparent because the system can show the information used to produce an answer.

An important challenge for AI research assistants is reliability. A generated answer may sound convincing even when the available evidence does not support it. For this reason, evidence retrieval and source tracking are useful parts of research-oriented AI systems.
